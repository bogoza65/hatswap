Save once. Switch forever.

No more logging out and in. No more five Chrome windows with five sets of bookmarks.

1

Save the login you're in

Sign in to a site the normal way, open Hatswap and click Save as New Account. Give it a name, a colour and a client.

2

Add the next one

New Account clears the site's login in your browser so you can sign in with the next account, then save that too.

3

Switch in one click

Click an account, or press ⌥⇧H. Hatswap swaps the session and brings you back to the page you were on. The badge shows who you are.

A saved login is a key. Treated like one.

Hatswap holds the sessions to your clients' accounts, so it was built to be checked, not trusted.

Stays on your computer

No account, no server, no telemetry. Hatswap makes no network requests at all. There is nothing to leak because nothing leaves.

Encrypted with your password

Every saved login is encrypted with AES-256 using a master password only you know. Hatswap locks itself after a time you choose.

Open source, per-site access

The code is public on GitHub. Access is granted one website at a time when you save your first account there, never to every site.

Honest limits. Hatswap handles cookies and local storage. A few apps keep their login elsewhere or tie it to the device; those won't switch. It switches a whole site at once, so it's made for business and client accounts, not for several personal profiles. Never click "Log out" inside a site for an account you've saved; use New Account instead. Details in the README.

Made for client work.

Names, colours and client groups per account. A badge in every tab. A guard that asks before saving the wrong login over a client's account.
